    How to use it
    1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer with Bluetooth turned on.
    2. Open chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features (in Edge edge://flags/…), set it to Enabled and restart the browser. Web Bluetooth still keeps advertisement scanning behind this flag.
    3. Wake the scale up by tapping it with your foot, then press Listen to the scale and pick the device in the browser's dialog (it may have no name).
    4. Step on and stand still until the reading turns final, about 10 seconds: the measurement is saved in this browser's history. The scale confirms the weight a few seconds after it stops changing.

    The scale does not accept connections: the browser listens to its advertisements. The phone app also sends it the time and an acknowledgement, which a browser cannot do; the scale broadcasts the weight anyway.

    Without a scale you can check the decoding with Replay a .pklg capture, which plays back a PacketLogger capture at 4× speed.
